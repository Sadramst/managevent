# Business plan (living document)

## One line

Managevent runs every kind of ceremony from 0 to 100 in Perth: planning, styling, hire, catering coordination, invitations and on-the-day delivery. It is powered by its own invitation and event software, then grows into a national marketplace. _A product by Appilico._

## The problem

- Hosts juggle 6 to 12 suppliers, spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups. Headcounts change constantly, and catering, cakes and seating are sized by guesswork.
- Cultural ceremonies are under-served by mainstream planners. That includes Persian (Sofreh Aghd, Baleh Boroon, Nowruz, Yalda, Mehmooni), Arabic, South Asian and Chinese ceremonies.
- Families arranging memorials need calm, competent help with the gathering itself (venue, wake catering, tribute displays) at a hard time.
- Digital invitations today are pretty pages, disconnected from fulfilment.

## Our answer

1. **Agency (phase 1):** fixed-price and custom-quoted 0-to-100 packages, delivered by our planners and vetted partners.
2. **Tools:** bilingual invitations with RSVP, dietary needs, QR check-in, photo wall and wishing well or tribute book. Free tier plus a Premium Event Pass.
3. **Marketplace (phase 2):** vetted vendors take bookings through us. We take commission via Stripe Connect.
4. **The bridge:** RSVP numbers automatically size catering, cake, seating and hire.

| Families (birthdays, anniversaries, showers, graduations) | Packages with clear prices, plus the hire catalogue                                 |

## Revenue streams

1. Ceremony packages: margin on services and hire, plus a planning fee.
2. Marketplace commission: a percentage of vendor bookings (target 10–15%, to be validated).
3. Invitation Premium Event Pass (one-off per event) and SMS or WhatsApp credits.
4. B2B subscriptions (corporate hosts) and premium vendor placement.

## Competitive edge

Bilingual and culturally specialised, transparent online estimates, owned software across the whole lifecycle, and Memorial Mode.

## Key risks

| Risk                               | Mitigation                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Supplier reliability               | Verification, backups for every category, run-sheets                    |
| Seasonality                        | Corporate end-of-year, Nowruz and Yalda peaks, memorials (not seasonal) |
| Holding customer money and refunds | Clear terms, deposits treated as liabilities, legal review              |
| Data and privacy                   | Australian hosting, minimal health data, retention                      |
| Founder bandwidth                  | Agents build the software, and a crew is hired by phase 3               |

## Key metrics

Leads per week, lead to booking conversion, average order value, gross margin per event, invitation sign-ups and Premium conversion, NPS after each event, vendor count and booking value (phase 2).
