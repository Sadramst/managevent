# Expansion

**Order:** Perth (WA) → Sydney (NSW) → Melbourne (VIC) → Brisbane (QLD) → Adelaide (SA) → Gold Coast (QLD).

## City readiness checklist

- [ ] Legal: state-specific liquor, food and WWCC equivalents reviewed. Update `COMPLIANCE_REGISTER.md` with the state rows.
- [ ] Time zone: daylight saving handling verified (NSW, VIC and SA observe it; QLD and WA do not).
- [ ] At least 10 verified vendors across the core categories. At least 8 venues in `content/locations/<city>/venues.json`.
- [ ] A city price list version, travel zones and areas served.
- [ ] Local SEO pages pass the thin-content guard. Then flip `city.json` status from `waitlist` to `live`.
- [ ] Crew: a local coordinator, or a marketplace-only launch where the agency service is not yet offered.
