# Go-to-market: Perth

Launch channels and timing. The strategy behind them is [PERTH_STRATEGY.md](PERTH_STRATEGY.md); the work items are in [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md).

## Launch sequence

1. **Soft launch** (website and enquiries): friends, family and local community networks. Deliver 3–5 showcase events at a discount in exchange for photos and testimonials, each with a consent record (`ConsentRecord` purposes `PHOTO_PUBLICATION` and `TESTIMONIAL`). Needs milestone M1.
2. **Public launch:** Google Business Profile, Instagram, community channels and partnerships with venues and caterers (BIZ-06, BIZ-07, BIZ-09). Local SEO pages for Perth suburbs are planned (MKT-10).
3. **Invitation-led growth:** every Free invitation carries the "Made with Managevent Invitations" credit linking to the invitation site. Prompting Premium hosts to get catering and hire quotes through the bridge needs the customer portal (PRT-04).

## Priority areas (travel zones)

Metro: Perth CBD, Stirling, Joondalup, Wanneroo, Swan, Bayswater, Canning, Melville, Fremantle, Cockburn, South Perth, Victoria Park. Outer metro: Armadale, Rockingham, Kalamunda and the Perth Hills, Mundaring. Regional is by quote only. The www estimator uses the same three zones (metro, outer, regional) with indicative surcharges (DEC-01).

## Seasonal calendar (Perth)

- **Weddings:** peak in spring and autumn (Sep–Nov, Mar–May).
- **Christmas and New Year parties:** start the campaign in October.
- **Corporate end-of-year:** Nov–Dec. Start the campaign in August.
- **Ramadan and Eid:** dates move each year. Iftar events.
