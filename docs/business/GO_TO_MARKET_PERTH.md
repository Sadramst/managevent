# Go-to-market: Perth

## Launch sequence

1. **Soft launch** (website, enquiries and Builder): friends, family and the Persian community network. Deliver 3–5 showcase events at a discount in exchange for photos and testimonials (with consent records).
2. **Public launch:** Google Business Profile, local SEO pages, Instagram (EN and FA), community WhatsApp and Telegram groups, partnerships with venues and caterers.
3. **Invitation-led growth:** free invitations carry a "Made with Managevent" link. Premium hosts are prompted to get catering and hire quotes through the bridge.

## Priority areas (travel zones)

Metro: Perth CBD, Stirling, Joondalup, Wanneroo, Swan, Bayswater, Canning, Melville, Fremantle, Cockburn, South Perth, Victoria Park. Outer metro: Armadale, Rockingham, Kalamunda and the Perth Hills, Mundaring. Regional is by quote only.

## Seasonal calendar (Perth)

- **Weddings:** peak in spring and autumn (Sep–Nov, Mar–May).
- **Nowruz:** around 20–21 March. Start the campaign in January.
- **Shab-e Yalda:** around 20–21 December. Start the campaign in October.
- **Corporate end-of-year:** Nov–Dec. Start the campaign in August.
- **Ramadan and Eid:** dates vary each year. Iftar events.
