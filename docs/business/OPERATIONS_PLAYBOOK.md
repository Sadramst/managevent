# Operations playbook (outline)

1. **Enquiry:** reply within 1 business hour. Qualify on date, guests, budget, ceremony and language.
2. **Consultation:** free, 15–45 min. Use the Builder estimate as the agenda.
3. **Proposal:** built in Admin from the estimate. Valid for 14 days. Contains the terms version.
4. **Booking:** deposit paid, then the event moves to `BOOKED`, the planner is assigned, and the portal invite is sent.
5. **Planning:** use the timeline template per ceremony. Vendor bookings get confirmations. Invitation sent around T-8 weeks (weddings) or T-3 weeks (parties).
6. **Final numbers:** at T-14 days, apply the RSVP-driven recommendations. Lock the order at T-7.
7. **Event pack:** at T-2 days, sync the crew devices and kiosk. Confirm the run-sheet with all vendors.
8. **Event day:** bump-in checklist, check-in, run-sheet, incident log, bump-out and inventory return scan.
9. **After:** within 24h, a thank-you, the photo wall zip and a review request. Vendor payouts released. Damage claims within 5 days.
10. **Close:** margin review, then retention timers start (privacy).

## Incident basics

- First aid kit and a trained crew member at every event. Emergency numbers on the run-sheet. Weather contingency for outdoor events.
- Allergy-severe guests are flagged to the caterer **by table**, not just by count.
