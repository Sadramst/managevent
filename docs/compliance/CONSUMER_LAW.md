# Australian Consumer Law: product rules

> Not legal advice. Terms and policies need legal review (especially unfair contract terms, which carry significant penalties).

## Engineering and product must

- Show **total prices including GST**. Don't drip in mandatory fees at checkout (travel zone fees must appear in the estimate).
- Keep "from" prices honest: they must be achievable.
- Not imply that consumer guarantees can be excluded. Refund policy text must not say "no refunds" in a way that misleads about statutory rights.
- Make deposits and cancellation fees reasonable, tied to actual costs incurred or committed. Show the cancellation schedule before payment.
- **Reviews and testimonials:** real customers only. No fake, edited-to-mislead or cherry-picked aggregate ratings. Disclose incentives. `Testimonial.verified` is required to publish.
- Use no fake urgency or scarcity ("Only 1 date left!") unless it is true and verifiable. No urgency anywhere in Memorial Mode.
- Show vendor terms and platform terms at signup, record the accepted version, and keep the terms fair (no unilateral variation without notice, etc.).
