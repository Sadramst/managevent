# Liquor and food safety (Western Australia)

> Verify with the WA Department of Local Government, Industry Regulation and Safety (Racing, Gaming and Liquor), local councils, and FSANZ.

## Liquor

- Selling alcohol (including tickets that include drinks) requires an appropriate licence or an **occasional licence**. Many venues hold their own licence; use their bar service.
- At private events where the host supplies alcohol free of charge, rules differ. Do not sell alcohol without a licence.
- **RSA:** all staff serving alcohol must hold current WA-recognised Responsible Service of Alcohol training. This is recorded in the crew profile and vendor documents.
- The Builder and Orders flag `alcoholService = true`, which requires RSA-certified staff in the staffing calculation and a licence check on the venue or vendor.

## Food

- Caterers must be registered food businesses with their local government (Food Act 2008, WA).
- **FSANZ Standard 3.2.2A** applies to most caterers (category one): a certified Food Safety Supervisor, food handler training and evidence records. We collect the certificates in vendor verification.
- Allergens are passed to caterers per table, with severity. Crew are briefed on any anaphylaxis risks.
- Managevent crew who handle food (for example plating cake or desserts) complete food handler training.
